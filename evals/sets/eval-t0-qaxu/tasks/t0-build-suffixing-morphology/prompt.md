The open project already has the stems, their meanings, and whether they are nouns or verbs. I have collected a set of additional word forms below. Please work out the regular noun and verb patterns, including which endings can occur in which order, and leave the changes ready for me to review. Keep the grammar as small as the evidence allows.

A few analyses I am confident about:

- usiswa = usis (ladle) + wa (PL)
- usisni = usis (ladle) + ni (LOC)
- tolenta = tolen (to wash) + ta (PST)
- tolenmu = tolen (to wash) + mu (1SG)

From my notes with speakers: when a noun has both endings, the plural `-wa` comes before the locative `-ni`; when a verb has both, the past `-ta` comes before the first-person `-mu`.
