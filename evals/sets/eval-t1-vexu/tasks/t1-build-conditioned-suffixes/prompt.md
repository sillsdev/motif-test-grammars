The open project already has the stems, their meanings, and whether they are nouns or verbs. I collected these additional forms. Some endings change shape according to the sound at the end of the stem. Please work out the regular noun and verb patterns and leave the changes ready for me to review. Use the examples to distinguish the forms and keep the grammar as small as the evidence allows.

A few analyses I am confident about:

- tifukta = tifuk (calabash) + ta (PL)
- tifukki = tifuk (calabash) + ki (LOC)
- lafetdu = lafet (to braid) + du (PST)
- lafetme = lafet (to braid) + me (1SG)

From my notes with speakers: when a noun has both endings, the plural comes before the locative `-ki`; when a verb has both, the past comes before the first-person `-me`.
